# M3 deferred and parked findings

These are review findings that were deliberately not fixed in M3. The per-task reviews (Sonnet) triaged them as "can wait", and the controller recorded them in the M3 ledger as deferred minors. Every such ledger entry, Tasks 1 to 25 and 24b, is listed below, including the first-review minors that the ledger only pointed to. Items struck through were closed by a later task, as noted; "carried" means the ledger handed the item to a later task. The final whole-branch review has not run yet. Use them as input when planning the next milestone. M2's list is in `m2-deferred-findings.md`.
